# Case D — fake revival calls: verified rule text

Only text read from the source PDFs (in life-corpus/raw/, git-ignored).
All read and checked on 27 Oct 2026 with `pdftotext -raw`.

## 1. Revival is done by the insurer, needs underwriting
IRDAI (Insurance Products) Regulations, 2024, Schedule I, 1.10:
> "Revival of a policy" means restoration of the policy, which was discontinued due to the non-payment of premium, by the insurer with all the benefits mentioned in the policy document, with or without rider benefits if any, upon the receipt of all the premiums due and other charges or late fee if any, during the revival period, as per the terms and conditions of the policy, upon being satisfied as to the continued insurability of the insured or policyholder on the basis of the information, documents and reports furnished by the policyholder, in accordance with Board approved underwriting policy.

## 2. Revival period
Same Regulations, Schedule I:
- 1.28 (linked): > "Revival period" means the period of three consecutive complete years from the date of first unpaid premium.
- 1.39 (non-linked): > "Revival period" means the period of five consecutive complete years from the date of first unpaid premium.

## 3. Linked (ULIP) revival: no interest or fee on due premiums
Master Circular on Life Insurance Products, 12 June 2024, Part C (linked products), para 40:
- 40.1.4.2.1 (revival during lock-in): > shall collect all due and unpaid premiums without charging any interest or fee;
- 40.1.4.2.2: may levy policy administration charge and premium allocation charge … guarantee charges … > No other charges shall be levied.
- 40.1.7.2.1 (after lock-in): > shall collect all due and unpaid premiums under base plan without charging any interest or fee.
- 40.1.7.2.3: > No other charges shall be levied.
**Linked products only.** For non-linked policies, 1.10 allows "other charges or late fee if any" per the policy terms — do NOT tell a non-linked policyholder that no fee is allowed.

## 4. IRDAI does not sell policies or announce bonuses
Master Circular on Insurance Advertisements, 16 Oct 2019 (IRDAI/LIFE/CIR/MISC/189/10/2019), para 15 — reproduces IRDAI's public notice. Extracted text (OCR is imperfect; quote only the clean span):
> is not involved in activities like selling insurance policies, announcing bonus or investment of premiums. Public receiving such phone calls are requested to lodge a police complaint.
Status after IRDAI's 2024 consolidation of circulars: NOT confirmed. Label it with its 2019 date on screen.

## Not supported by anything read so far — do not claim
- "Pay only to the insurer / never pay an agent." Not stated in these texts. Use instead: revival is "by the insurer … upon the receipt of all the premiums due" (1.10) and tell the user to pay only through the insurer's official channels as a safety step, labelled as advice, not as a rule.
- Whether a specific policy exists or its status. Only the insurer knows. Always direct to the insurer's official website/app/branch, or Bima Bharosa.
- Any named insurer as a scam source in demo or public material.
